- **Network mode control**: Signal → Band Control gains an
  Automatic / 4G LTE only / 5G NSA / 5G SA selector, applied together with the
  band masks in one verified write (masks first, then the RAT mode preference,
  with best-effort rollback on failure). 4G LTE only disables 5G while keeping
  your NR selections for when you switch back; 5G NSA offers every LTE band as
  anchor candidates and empties the SA mask; 5G SA drops LTE entirely
  (standalone). If 5G SA never attaches, SA may still be disabled at firmware
  level (`nr5g_disable_mode` — see device.md).
- **Restore original** now also reverts the RAT mode preference captured in the
  band baseline; baselines written by older versions restore bands only.
